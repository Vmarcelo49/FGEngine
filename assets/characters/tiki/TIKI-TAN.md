# Tiki-Tan & KACHI — the $5 Curse

## Concept
The regular fighting-game loli. The classic "cliche anime little girl with giant thing"

- **Hair:** long blonde, reaches her back
- **Outfit:** oversized orange hoodie + blue/green pleated mini skirt + white thigh-highs with orange trim + sneakers
- **Signature pose:** one hand ALWAYS holding her helmet so it doesn't fall off
- **Most notable feature:** oversized magical totem helmet with a primitive face, leaking jade-green spirits she uses for attacks

## the plot
Hunting for a cheap cool costume prop for an event, Tiki-tan let a shady seller talk her into a $5 "replica" totem helmet.

It was the real thing a cursed vessel used for malicious rites thousands of years ago.

She doesn't know that. She just knows the green spirits obey her now, and with that giant helmet slipping over her eyes she feels unbeatable — so she picks a fight with anyone in her way.

## Naming
Kept the original `helmet` idea and did a mythology + pun split:

- **TIKI-TAN** = Tiki (Polynesian totem carving) + -tan (moe honorific for little girls, cuter than -chan). Double pun: she's tan/orange from her hoodie.
  - This is what *she* calls herself for the cosplay event, her real name is another pun that i didnt think yet.
- **KACHI** = from Kachina (Hopi spirit dolls — living spirits inside carved heads, exactly what he is). Short, grumpy, ancient.
  - The totem's real name. 1000-year-old malicious curse god, furious he was sold for $5 and stuck on a kid's head being called "Kachi-chan".
  - He does the actual fighting. He lets her believe *she's* controlling the spirits.

Full title card: **TIKI-TAN & KACHI, the $5 Curse**
I must also reference the $19 fortnite card somehow

## Duo Dynamic
- Tiki-tan: hyper, overconfident, idiot gremlin energy. Thinks she's unbeatable.
- Kachi: grumpy old god, glowing eyes when he's annoyed. Whispers combo inputs. Complains constantly.
- Comedy core: the helmet is WAY bigger than her head and constantly slipping. most of her attacks are based on either energy spirits or the helmet being thrown around.
